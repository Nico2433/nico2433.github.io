Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

"rclone personal" is a personal-use application. It is used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive account through rclone running on their own computer.

Data accessed

The application accesses Google Drive files solely to copy, sync and back them up between Google Drive and the owner's computer.

Data storage and sharing

No data is collected, stored on external servers, sold or shared with any third party. Authentication tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the owner through GitHub.
